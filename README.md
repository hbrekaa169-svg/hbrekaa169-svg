## 📌 About My Work

I specialize in **computational linguistics** and **Arabic language processing**, with a particular focus on how Large Language Models generate Arabic text and why standard AI outputs often fail academic rigor and stylometric tests.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Creator and maintainer of **[Egyptian Humanizer 2027](https://github.com/hbrekaa169-svg/egyptian-humanize-app-2027)** — a high-performance engine that restructures flat LLM drafts into nuanced scholarly Arabic compliant with APA 7th standards.
- 🧠 **Research Interests:** Syntactic burstiness, lexical entropy, and overcoming repetitive token patterns flagged by AI detection systems (Turnitin, GPTZero, CopyLeaks).
- 🔒 **Design Philosophy:** Local-first, lightweight, and sovereign. Software should run on your hardware without leaking research manuscripts to external servers.
